About me

Hi, I'm Omar

I'm an aerospace engineer in Munich. Since April 2026 I've worked in the Orbital Systems department at deltaVision, on refuelling hardware for multi-orbit spacecraft.

My role sits between mechanical design and systems engineering: physical architecture, CAD and FEA, with an eye on how each design decision travels through the rest of the system. That interest runs through my path so far, from a BEng in Mechanical Engineering at Coventry University to an MSc in Aerospace at TUM, systems engineering at Rocket Factory Augsburg, thermal-vacuum hardware at TUM's Lunar and Planetary Exploration group, and a master's thesis designing a self-refuelling mission to Enceladus.

I'm most interested in what comes next for in-space mobility: refuelling, servicing and the logistics networks that make spacecraft manoeuvrable for their whole lives.
