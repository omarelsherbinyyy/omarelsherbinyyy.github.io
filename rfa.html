Internship · Jun – Aug 2025

Systems engineering at Rocket Factory Augsburg

Rocket Factory Augsburg, founded in 2018 as a spin-off of the OHB Group, develops launch vehicles for low-cost access to space. For three months I worked in its Systems Engineering department.

Details

What I worked on

  • Developed a recursive, system-level risk assessment tool: a probabilistic formula that combines prior knowledge with test data to evaluate the reliability of safety-critical systems and subsystems.
  • Analysed test data and applied the risk assessment and safety analysis to the high-pressure gas storage system (COPVs).
  • Ran an FMEA on the launcher separation system.
  • Compiled test data on solenoid and actuated valves to quantify their actuation history during testing.
  • Documented the architecture differences between the first and second stage.
  • Reviewed and catalogued 21 ground launch systems, including propellant handling and storage, pressurisation, spin-start, water deluge, hydraulics and umbilicals.

Seeing how system-level decisions propagate down to components, and how component performance feeds back into the architecture, was my most valuable professional experience so far.
