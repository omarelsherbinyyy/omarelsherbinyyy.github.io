Master's thesis · ENCORE

A mission to Enceladus that refuels itself

ENCORE is a candidate ESA L-Class mission: an orbiter and a hopping lander that turn Enceladus ice into propellant with water electrolysis. My thesis covers the trajectory, the hybrid chemical-electric propulsion system and the system architecture.
